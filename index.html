Questions

What people ask us

Don’t see yours? Call the office and ask a licensed electrician.

What kind of electrical work do you do?

Service and repairs, panel upgrades, EV chargers, pot lights and lighting, older wiring, new residential and commercial construction, underground electrical, bucket truck work and SmartSpace systems — for homes and for businesses.

Where do you work?

Our Moncton office covers Moncton, Dieppe, Riverview, Shediac, Sackville, Salisbury and the surrounding area. Our office in Charlottetown covers all of Prince Edward Island.

Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We believe in clear, upfront pricing: a licensed electrician looks at the work and walks you through what it needs and what it costs, so you decide before we start.

Are your electricians licensed?

Yes. LuMar Electric is fully licensed and insured, our team includes Red Seal electricians, and our safety program is COR® certified.

Can I be served in French?

Yes. Our team serves customers in English and in French, in New Brunswick and on PEI.
