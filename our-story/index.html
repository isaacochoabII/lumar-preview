Our Story

From a small garage to 140 employees, LuMar Electric’s story is one of family, ambition, and unwavering dedication. Discover how two cousins built a trusted name in electrical services across New Brunswick and Prince Edward Island.

In their words

From the first job to 140 employees

Luc and Mario sat down at Leven Press Studios to tell the story themselves.

Family Roots and Early Inspiration

Cousins Luc (Lu) Chiasson and Mario (Mar) Leblanc grew up together, but it wasn’t until adulthood that their paths officially crossed in business.

Both inspired by Luc’s father, an experienced electrician, they entered the trade early. Mario, while still in high school, would often spend afternoons helping Luc on job sites.

Their passion for the craft led them to forgo the traditional college route and dive straight into hands-on apprenticeships.

Gaining Experience and a Bigger Vision

After gaining years of industry experience separately, including time working out west in Calgary, Luc and Mario returned home.

They operated their own independent contracting businesses but often collaborated. It didn’t take long before they realized they could build something bigger together.

The Birth of LuMar Electric

In 2011, LuMar Electric was born. The name, a blend of “Lu” and “Mar,” was Mario’s idea.

They started humbly, setting up shop in Luc’s garage — proudly referring to it as “the shop” even though it was a modest 15-by-20-foot space.

From the start, they believed in branding and professionalism, plastering the LuMar logo on trucks and equipment.

Hustle, Cold Calls, and First Wins

The early days were fueled by coffee, cold calls, and a lot of hustle.

Luc and Mario worked tirelessly to build relationships, winning clients not just with competitive bids but with genuine enthusiasm and follow-through.

They faced a highly competitive market, but what they lacked in size, they made up for in persistence and passion.

Landing their first large apartment building project was a turning point. Although daunting, they embraced the challenge, believing that the only way to grow was to step into bigger opportunities — even before they felt fully ready.

Strategic Growth and Diversification

Over time, LuMar expanded, adding departments for residential, commercial, multi-unit development, and service & maintenance.

This diversification allowed them to keep employees working even during market slowdowns.

A Team Built on Loyalty and Trust

Today, LuMar Electric is proud to have a team of 140 talented individuals.

Many of their early employees are still with the company — a testament to the strong culture of loyalty, growth, and mutual respect that Luc and Mario fostered from day one.

At LuMar, success is measured not just by the number of projects completed but by the community they’ve built and the opportunities they’ve created for their team.
