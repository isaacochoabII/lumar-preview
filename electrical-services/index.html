Overview

Trusted electrical services across NB and PEI

At LuMar Electric, we deliver trusted electrical services across New Brunswick and Prince Edward Island. With four specialized divisions—Residential, Commercial, Electrical Service & Maintenance, and SmartSpace Solutions—we provide expert support for projects of every size and complexity. Whether you’re building, upgrading, maintaining, or modernizing, we have the skills and experience to get the job done right.
