Our SmartSpace Solutions

Smarter Spaces Start Here.

At LuMar Electric, our SmartSpace Division specializes in modern electrical systems that bring comfort, control, and energy efficiency to your home or business. From smart lighting and home automation to advanced building controls and integrated energy-saving systems, we design and install solutions that make your space more connected, functional, and future-ready.

Smart Lighting Systems

Home & Business Automation

Energy-Efficient Upgrades

Panel Integration & Wiring

Questions

What people ask us

Don’t see yours? Call the office and ask a licensed electrician.

What does SmartSpace install?

Smart lighting control, home and business automation, intercoms and door access, security cameras and CCTV, structured cabling and fibre optics, and wireless networks.

Is it for homes or businesses?

Both. SmartSpace works for residential and commercial clients, in new construction and in renovations.

Can it work with Google Home, Alexa or Control4?

Yes. Automation can integrate with platforms like Google Home, Alexa or Control4.

Can I buy the pieces separately?

Yes. Every package is available individually and fully customizable.
