Permanent Outdoor Lighting

Installed once, customizable all year

LuMar Electric has partnered with Watts Lights to bring you the latest in permanent smart outdoor lighting.

Installed once by our licensed electricians, your system gives you beautiful lighting you can customize all year long—in your favourite sports team’s colours or for any occasion, from Christmas and Halloween to backyard parties, game nights and everyday curb appeal.
