What It’s Like to Work at LuMar Electric

At LuMar Electric, we’re committed to helping our team grow and succeed. We offer a collaborative, supportive environment where innovation is valued and hard work is rewarded. We’re looking for skilled, motivated professionals who are passionate about delivering high-quality work and ready to power the future with us.

When you join LuMar Electric, you can expect:

  • Full-time, year-round employment
  • Competitive wages
  • Health and dental benefits
  • Vision care
  • Pension Plan
  • Paid vacation
  • Work clothing & tool allowance
  • A positive, team-focused workplace

Residential Electrician

Moncton·Full-Time

Experience / Certifications: 1st to 4th year apprentices, licensed Journeyperson Electricians (Red Seal or equivalent) and experienced foremen. Residential experience preferred (2 years). A valid driver’s licence is required. Reliable, safety-focused, and just as comfortable working independently as part of a team.

Apply Now

Commercial Electrician or Foreman

Moncton·Full-Time

Experience / Certifications: 2nd or 3rd year apprentices, licensed Journeyperson Electricians (Red Seal or equivalent) or forepersons to join our commercial division and run crews. You can read and interpret electrical drawings and specifications. Commercial or multi-unit residential experience is preferred, and conduit, cable tray, lighting systems or large-scale installations are a plus. A valid driver’s licence is required.

Apply Now

Service & Maintenance Foreman (Electrician)

Moncton·Full-Time

Experience / Certifications: A licensed Journeyperson Electrician (Red Seal or equivalent) with residential and/or commercial service experience. You respond to service calls, diagnose electrical issues and complete repairs and installations from a company service vehicle. A strong troubleshooter, comfortable working independently, with a valid driver’s licence and a clean driving record.

Apply Now

Service & Maintenance Electrician (Block 3 Apprentice)

Moncton·Full-Time

Experience / Certifications: A Block 3 Apprentice Electrician with residential and/or commercial experience. You work alongside an experienced Service & Maintenance Foreman as a two-person team on troubleshooting, repairs, installations and maintenance. Good problem-solving skills and a strong willingness to learn; a valid driver’s licence is an asset.

Apply Now

Construction Electrician – PEI

Charlottetown, PEI·Full-Time

Experience / Certifications: 1st to 4th year apprentices and Red Seal Journeypersons across our Residential, Multi-Unit Commercial and Service departments. Electrical experience preferred (1 year). A valid driver’s licence is required.
