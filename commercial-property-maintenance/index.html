Commercial service

Rarely just an electrical problem

When something electrical fails in a commercial building, it is rarely just an electrical problem. It is a storefront trading in the dark, a warehouse shift standing still, or a tenant on the phone waiting for an answer you do not have yet.

LuMar Electric’s Repairs & Maintenance department handles that work for commercial property — troubleshooting, repairs, panel upgrades, lighting and preventative maintenance, carried out with minimal disruption to your operations.

We run two offices, in Moncton and Charlottetown, so a portfolio that spans both provinces is one contractor rather than two. Licensed, insured, and established in 2011.

Who we work with

Built for whoever is responsible for the building

Our Commercial Department works across retail spaces, offices, warehouses and multi-unit developments in Moncton, Charlottetown and the surrounding areas. Different buildings, same problem: something needs to work again, and it needs to happen without shutting the place down.

Property Managers

You answer to tenants who need it fixed today and owners who need it done properly. One call covers the portfolio rather than one unit — rentals, multi-unit developments and the common areas in between.

Storefronts & Retail

A dark sign or a dead circuit is lost trade. We repair interior and exterior lighting, signage circuits and retail power — and our bucket truck reaches the signs, pole lighting and service masts that a ladder will not.

Warehouses & Distribution

Power distribution, panel capacity and high-bay lighting for space that runs long hours. Those same long hours are where an LED retrofit pays back fastest.

Offices & Multi-Unit Buildings

Lighting, panel upgrades, EV chargers for staff and tenant parking, and everyday troubleshooting. For intercoms, access control and CCTV, our SmartSpace division handles the low-voltage side.

What we handle

This is day-to-day electrical service work — the things that need fixing, upgrading or servicing without a full construction plan behind them.

  • Troubleshooting & diagnostics — flickering lights, tripped breakers, sudden power loss
  • Electrical repairs & replacements — outlets, switches, damaged wiring, failed circuits
  • Panel upgrades & breaker fixes where the service no longer meets the load
  • Lighting repairs & LED retrofits, interior and exterior
  • Bucket truck work for signs, pole lighting and service masts
  • EV charger installations at businesses and rental properties
  • Preventative maintenance and electrical system inspections

Planning a build rather than fixing one? New construction, tenant fit-outs and developer work are handled by our New Commercial Construction team.
